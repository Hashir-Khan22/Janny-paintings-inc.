Industrial and Commercial Painters

Since 1982, Janney Painting Inc. has established an unyielding reputation as one of the Mid-Atlantic’s leading heavy-industrial painting and abrasive sandblasting contractors. For over 43 years, we have specialized in the structural restoration of heavy industrial facilities, grain handling operations, aggregate mills, and chemical storage systems.

Whether your project requires abrasive blast-cleaning to SSPC-SP 10 Near-White standards, lead paint encapsulation, or high-performance urethane topcoats, our MSHA Part 46 certified crews arrive fully equipped with our own specialized cranes, compressors, and containment rigging.
